About

Researching environmental change across rivers, landscapes, and communities.

I am an Environmental Science researcher at Khulna University, Bangladesh, interested in understanding environmental change through the combined use of remote sensing, GIS, field evidence, and quantitative analysis.

My research has progressively moved from coastal land-use change and ecosystem-service assessment toward integrated river-corridor analysis and computational geomorphology. My MSc research examines three decades of river morphological change, land-use transitions, and household livelihood vulnerability along the Rupsha–Pasur River corridor. My current work extends this direction by investigating spatially offset curvature–migration relationships using multi-decadal Landsat observations and hierarchical Bayesian modeling.

I aim to further develop geospatial, computational, and statistical approaches for studying riverine and coastal environmental change through graduate research.

Current Research · Manuscript in Preparation

Spatially Offset Curvature–Migration Coupling in the Tidal Rupsha–Pasur River Corridor from Multi-Decadal Satellite Observations

This project examines whether antecedent channel curvature is associated with subsequent lateral migration locally or at upstream distances scaled to channel width. It combines Landsat-derived channel geometry, signed curvature, mapped-centerline migration, measurement-uncertainty analysis, and hierarchical Bayesian modeling.
