02 / Results

Key findings

  1. A one-standard-deviation increase in residential NDVI was associated with 9% lower adjusted odds of perinatal depression (OR 0.91; 95% CI 0.842–0.977).
  2. Ambient temperature was positively associated with perinatal depression (adjusted OR 1.44; 95% CI 1.290–1.625).
  3. The study reported variations across demographic and geographic subgroups; interaction patterns were exploratory.

04 / Methods

Study approach

Demographic and Health Survey data (Bangladesh, Mozambique, Nepal), NDVI/EVI, and generalized linear mixed-effects logistic regression.

05 / Careful interpretation

Interpretation: A cross-sectional observational association does not demonstrate that greenness prevents perinatal depression.

Limitations

The observational design identifies associations, not causal protective effects of greenness.
