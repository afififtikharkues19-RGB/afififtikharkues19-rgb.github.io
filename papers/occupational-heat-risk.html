Occupational heat risk perceptions and behavioral adaptation strategies among construction and welding workers in Bangladesh

02 / Results

Key findings

  1. More than 80% of participants perceived themselves as vulnerable to occupational heat.
  2. Compared with welding workers, construction workers were more likely to report selected adaptations, including taking breaks and wearing loose clothing.
  3. Adaptation patterns differed with work experience and reported symptoms, indicating a need for targeted heat-safety interventions.

04 / Methods

Study approach

Questionnaire survey of 320 workers in Dhaka, Khulna, and Barishal; multinomial logistic regression.

05 / Careful interpretation

Interpretation: Self-reported cross-sectional comparisons and wide confidence intervals limit causal interpretation.

Limitations

Self-reported, cross-sectional findings have limited causal interpretation; some odds-ratio confidence intervals are wide.
