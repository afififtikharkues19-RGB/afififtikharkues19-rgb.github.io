02 / Results

Key findings

  1. The assessed ecological risk was generally low to moderate, with Hg and As contributing disproportionately to risk metrics.
  2. Localized enrichment of As, Zn, and Pb indicated emerging anthropogenic pressures.
  3. Receptor and multivariate analyses indicated that natural sedimentary processes predominated, alongside more localized anthropogenic contributions.

04 / Methods

Study approach

INAA and AAS measurements; contamination and ecological-risk indices; PCA, HCA, correlation and positive matrix factorization.

05 / Careful interpretation

Interpretation: The four source factors are model-based interpretations, not direct source tracing or proof of particular facilities.

Limitations

The source factors are modeled interpretations, not direct identification of individual emissions facilities.
