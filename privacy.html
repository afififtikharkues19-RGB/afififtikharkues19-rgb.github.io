Privacy & visitor analytics

This academic portfolio is designed to present research, publications and professional work. The following describes the optional analytics integration prepared for this site.

What may be measured

If analytics is enabled, GoatCounter receives page-visit information, approximate country and referral information according to its service settings, and selected aggregate link events (CV download links, research repository links and publication DOI links). A click does not establish a completed download or indicate who clicked.

The portfolio does not include personal identifiers in custom event names, does not attempt to identify individual visitors, and does not send contact-form details or research participant data. GoatCounter's processing and session counting are described in its privacy documentation. Visits are estimates, not a count of distinct people.

Your browser preference

When analytics is active, the integration respects Global Privacy Control, Do Not Track, and the local opt-out preference below. No analytics requests are sent by this privacy page itself.

Checking browser preference…

The opt-out setting is stored only in this browser; clearing local storage removes it. The setting can be changed at any time. Analytics may also be blocked by browser extensions.

Questions

Contact the portfolio owner via the contact page with privacy or research-access questions. Access to aggregate analytics should remain restricted to the site owner.
