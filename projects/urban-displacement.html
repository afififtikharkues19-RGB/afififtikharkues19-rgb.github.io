Research assistantship / 2024

Urban Displacement Research

Satkhira and Bagerhat, Bangladesh

Mixed-methods investigation of climate-linked displacement and integration pressures in secondary cities.

Project findingClimate stresses and livelihood losses shaped migration experiences

8focus group discussions
16key-informant interviews
10in-depth case studies

01 / Central result

What the evidence shows

The study linked reported environmental pressures and agricultural livelihood decline with migration into towns where limited housing and social services compounded vulnerability.

Position / project groupResearch Assistant — Khulna University

IFRC / Bangladesh Red Crescent / American Red Cross-supported

02 / Findings

Key findings

  1. Reported drivers combined climatic pressures and economic circumstances, including agricultural livelihood decline.
  2. The study identified housing, service access and institutional capacity challenges for displaced households.
  3. My contribution included FGD/KII instrument development, thematic analysis and synthesis of qualitative findings from FGDs, KIIs and case studies.

My contribution

I contributed to developing FGD/KII research instruments, thematic analysis, and synthesis of findings from focus groups, key-informant interviews and in-depth case studies.
