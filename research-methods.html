BSc dissertation · 1993–2023

How sensitive are ecosystem-service estimates to valuation assumptions?

Published value coefficients turn land-cover areas into modeled monetary estimates. Their transferability matters, especially when locally important land uses have no directly corresponding coefficient.

  • Uncertainty from all sources through the one-at-a-time sensitivity test.
