FEATURED RESEARCH · Manuscript in preparation

Spatially Offset Curvature–Migration Associations in a Tidal River Corridor

Multi-Decadal Evidence from the Rupsha–Pasur · 1988–2025

The question: can curvature measured upstream of a point help explain later signed mapped-centerline migration in a tide-influenced channel? I tested six width-scaled spatial offsets using Landsat-derived geometry and a hierarchical Bayesian Student-t model.

Explore the complete curvature–migration study ↗
Clearest global association2.0B

Two local channel widths upstream; a small, noncausal outward-aligned association (−0.085 m yr⁻¹ per +1 SD κB).

Only 2.0B had a global 95% credible interval excluding zero. At that offset, 10/12 local posterior means were negative, but no individual block 95% interval excluded zero. Results are observational and subject to mapping and model uncertainty.

Healthcare carbon footprint assessment

The study assessed greenhouse-gas sources and mitigation pathways across nine facilities at three tiers of healthcare.

PROJECT FINDINGS

Reported three-month footprints ranged from 18.28 to 93.61 t CO₂-e among the three examined upazila facilities and from 1,663.32 to 2,688.64 t CO₂-e among the three tertiary hospitals.

Social services access — GIZ INTEGRATE

Across five cities, the report found that awareness of benefits often did not translate into successful access.

PROJECT FINDINGS
